# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities in our website, apps, or repositories privately to [security@beastinct.com](mailto:security@beastinct.com), not in a public issue.

Test minimally: no overload, no downloading or changing other people's data, no social engineering. Do not test client systems without their permission; we forward reports to the owner.

Response times and what we do with your data, code, and AI: [beastinct.com/en/security](https://beastinct.com/en/security).
